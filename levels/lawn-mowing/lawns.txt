# Booth lawns for "Rasenmähen", drawn on the integer grid (one character per unit cell, top row first).
#   #  lawn (must be mowed)
#   .  outside the lawn
#   S  hole: a small paved square (1 x 1 or 2 x 2)
#   W  hole: a paved path ("Weg", a strip 1 or 2 cells wide, at least 3 long)
#   T  hole: a terrace (any other shape)
# Holes are stone slabs (Kevin, 2026-10-19: no ponds, trees or beds, which visitors steered round): they
# need no mowing, and cutters drive over them and may leave the lawn. The game names a hole by its shape
# by the same rule, so the letters only document the intent (the generator checks they agree).
# `python3 scripts/lawns/make_instances.py` turns every lawn into pyutils27 instances with a 3 x 3
# cutter for 1 and 2 cutters in levels/lawn-mowing/instances/.
